## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno will be tracking the customer feedback from discovery meetings, maintaining a synthesis of that feedback, tracking the most relevant and critical points that are raised by our customers to look at the backlog and give recommendations on current priorities for the team. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Juno will have access to all the customer meeting transcripts and the teams backlog

**How fresh must it be**

she will check every two weeks when the PM is doing Sprint Planning

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

It can read the customer meeting information and synthesize the feedback into prioritize and product direction. It can go into the backlog and check what the team is working on , ask questions to clarify what tickets actually are addressing for the customer needs and then give the product team an overview of how it is evaluating priorities and work items.

**Deliberate omissions**

Ignore Jira tickets that are for the backend or system maintenance

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

It gets one pass every week, and can do more when the PM asks it to.

**Latency and cost target**

When new customer feedback is added, don't go through the old one again, just take the new information and compare with existing database

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Memory of how the team manages the backlog, and the core focuses of the customer feedback sessions so that she can keep track of how they are changing. It should also remember what user profiles said what so we can focus on the main target persona

**Expiry and write rules**

The PM decides when facts expire, but Juno asks every two months if she should keep it or get rid of it

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

It can review the anonymized customer feedback meetings and the team's Jira backlog

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

The PM reviews the suggestions and accepts or rejects them once every week

**Failure behaviour**

When the system fails to provide feedback on the backlog based on customer feedback it should flag it clearly in its biweekly run and explain why
